I am a machine learning engineer and applied scientist based in San Francisco.

These days I work on reinforcement learning and large language models at Amazon's SF AI Lab, where I helped found the RL group and focus on CoT/RLVR training and agentic systems. Over the past decade I've built ML systems at Adept, Stripe, Twitch, and Google — spanning computer-use agents, risk and fraud detection, video delivery, and real-time ad bidding. Before all that I studied mathematics at UCLA and Northwestern.

I like turning fuzzy, open-ended problems into systems that actually ship. When I'm not doing that, I'm usually tinkering with the small pile of software that runs my life — some of which ends up on this site.

You can read more in my CV or browse the blog.
